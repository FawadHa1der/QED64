Loading Lean 4 + Mathlib into your browser

First visit downloads about 1 GB (the Lean runtime and the full Mathlib environment) and caches it in your browser — later visits start in about 20 seconds. Everything then runs locally: no servers, and your proofs never leave this tab. The full Mathlib environment needs ~6 GB of memory — on smaller machines, close other heavy tabs first.
