Loading Lean 4 + Mathlib into your browser

First visit downloads about 460 MB (the Lean runtime and a curated Mathlib environment) and caches it in your browser — later visits start in a few seconds. Everything then runs locally: no servers, and your proofs never leave this tab. With the Mathlib environment loaded, this tab uses about 2.5 GB of memory — on machines with 8 GB or less, close other heavy tabs first.
