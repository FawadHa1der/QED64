Loading Lean 4 + Mathlib into your browser

First visit downloads about 600 MB (the Lean runtime and the full Mathlib environment) and caches it in your browser — later visits start in about 20 seconds. Everything then runs locally: no servers, and your proofs never leave this tab. With the full Mathlib environment loaded, this tab uses about 8–9 GB of memory — on machines with 16 GB or less, close other heavy tabs first.
