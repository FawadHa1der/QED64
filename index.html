About

QED64 runs a genuine LP64 build of the Lean 4 compiler as WebAssembly Memory64 with pthreads — proofs are elaborated and kernel-checked on your machine, never on a server. Runtime and libraries are content-addressed and SHA-256-verified before they execute. Requires desktop Chrome/Edge 133+ or Firefox 134+.

This deployment runs the QED64 clean-room runtime, which includes toolchain patch 0010: parse errors (unreadable text, unterminated declarations) are reported with positions, like every other diagnostic. The editor additionally pre-flags parser-skipped lines instantly, before the compiler even runs.
